INFERRED, NOT_VERIFIED. Severity: P0 (security / data-loss / cross-tenant / unsafe ad execution), P1
(production blocker or serious financial/representation risk), P2 (substantial functional/reliability

## 3. Verdicts

### 3.1 Persona fit — I concur with 07/G7: **no persona reaches WORKS.**
The product is positioned as an Arabic-first "AI media buyer," but the single keystone truth — the AI never
analyses the tenant's accounts in either mode (SC1) — means every persona's *core value* is a demo. One
persona (small single-currency advertiser) can extract partial, read-only reporting value; the other four
(agency, enterprise, e-commerce brand, 50-account buyer) are structurally blocked. I vouch for the 07
persona scorecard without change. The agency and 50-account personas are the most emphatically blocked
(flat single-org model, no switcher, one grant/provider, cross-tenant report list, org-wide RBAC, no bulk
ops), and the Agency tier is sold on a feature (`clientWorkspaces`) that does not exist (SC3).

### 3.2 Onboarding — **functional but mis-targeted; it engineers an immediate activation failure.**
Onboarding persists state and gates steps correctly (VERIFIED by G4's 57 passing tests), and the i18n layer
is genuinely strong. But it is written for the upstream developer audience: the terminal step makes the
buyer wire an MCP server into ChatGPT/Cursor (SC7) and never introduces the product's own Assistant. Worse,
a new signup lands on Free/reader, whose entitlement strips `tools:write`, so they cannot even *preview* a
write (SC12) — while the welcome copy promises "every write requires an exact preview." The REST path then
returns a raw 403. An invited teammate who already owns a personal org can never reach the workspace they
were invited to (oldest-membership resolver, SC4). Verdict: the onboarding *mechanism* is sound; the
*journey* ends in a 403 or an empty dashboard for the default persona on the default plan.

### 3.3 Arabic quality — **chrome is production-grade; the AI's own output is not, and that undercuts the positioning.**
The deliberate calls are correct for a Gulf media buyer: Arabic default locale, RTL document direction,
six-category plural handling, Latin-digit numerals (`test/i18n.test.ts` 8/8). Against that, four real
problems: (a) terminology was written module-by-module with no glossary (SC10) — this is the first thing a
native reviewer flags, but the proposed glossary is a *proposal*, not a verdict, and needs a native Saudi
media buyer to ratify; (b) one genuinely functional defect — timestamps are UTC with a hard-coded suffix,
so an approval that expires 17:00 Riyadh displays 14:00 UTC (P2, misread approval windows); (c) typography/
RTL polish (mono/uppercase/tracking on every label, a visible billing-checkmark overlap, bidi reordering of
mixed lines, English browser-tab titles) — P3, and the RUNTIME evidence is inherited from G3's screenshots,
which I did not re-run; (d) the most strategically important gap: everything the *AI media buyer actually
says* — analysis, proposals, refusal reasons, report PDFs — is English, because no locale is forwarded to
the engine and `engine/instructions.md` has no language directive. For an "Arabic-first" product, the thing
the customer came for speaks English. Verdict: ship-quality Arabic *shell*; the Arabic *brain* is a GAP.

### 3.4 The "AI Media Buyer Workbench" IA proposal — **right north star; wrong to build before the keystone fix.**
The proposed IA (Ask-AI as the spine, Morning Brief home, Account Health, Opportunities/Recommendations,
Approvals, Decisions & Outcomes; Analyze group; Settings group) is sound and — importantly — maps onto
engine capabilities that genuinely exist: cadence machinery (`engine/.../reports/cadence.py`),
`compare_periods`, the `CREATIVE` entity level, and the `audit_run` findings producer. I endorse it as the
product's direction. Two rulings attach: (1) **Sequencing** — the Workbench is almost entirely
"differentiator later." Building Morning Brief / Account Health / Opportunities on top of an engine pinned
to fixtures (SC1) would produce a prettier demo, not a product. The tenant-data path and the working write
loop must land *first*; the Workbench is what you build *on* them. (2) **Correction** — the proposal says
to demote Findings out of primary nav "until a producer is wired." A producer already exists (`audit_run`,
SC6); the correct fix is an in-dashboard "Run audit" action that calls it, not demotion. The IA is
otherwise adopted wholesale into my recommendations.

## 4. Must exist before the first paying customer vs. differentiator later

This is the central ruling of my seat. "Must exist" = you cannot honestly charge a customer without it
(core value is absent, a paid claim is false, a core flow is broken, a legal/representation line is
crossed, or margin is unprotected at scale). "Differentiator later" = real value that makes the product
competitive but whose absence is a gap, not a block, for a first flat-priced customer.

### MUST EXIST before the first paying customer
| # | Item | Why it blocks charging | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | **Tenant data path** — the AI must analyse the customer's real connected accounts | The product *is* "AI analyses your accounts"; today it analyses fixtures in both modes and the UI labels them "your connected accounts" | K7-01 (P1) |
| M2 | **Working write loop** without hand-written SQL (alias-management UI) | Any plan that sells `writeAccess` (Operator+) has no usable AI→write path in production | K7-02 (P1) |
| M3 | **Stop selling absent features** — remove or implement `clientWorkspaces` (Agency) and SSO/regional-hosting/SLA (Enterprise) | Live Stripe checkout bills them; charging for a non-existent feature is a representation/refund risk | K7-03, K7-04 (P1) |
| M4 | **Own legal identity** — privacy/terms/support/enterprise-CTA under markting-ai, not a third party | Published data controller is "Yannick Westermann"; the enterprise lead and support route to the upstream vendor | K7-05 (P1) |
| M5 | **Honest plan disclosure + no raw 403** on the default plan; fix the preview-on-Free trap | The default persona completes onboarding and immediately fails | K7-07 (P2) |
| M6 | **Fix the headline ROAS tile** (request `conversion_value`) and label currency | A media-buying product whose headline efficiency metric is always 0× has no credibility | K7-06 (P2) |
| M7 | **Fix "Remove member"** | Shipped-broken core team flow | K7-08 (P2) |
| M8 | **Per-tenant report scoping** before any real engine data | Unscoped `listReports()` becomes a P0 cross-tenant leak the moment reports carry real data; also blocks any agency customer | K7-12 (P1 / INFERRED-P0) |
| M9 | **A basic AI-usage guard** (per-plan cap or at least observability) | Unbounded LLM/engine cost on every tier including Free — margin is unprotected | K7-16 (P1 financial, partial) |

| M11 | **Assistant thread persistence** (the hero surface should not lose state on reload) | The Assistant is the product; losing the conversation on refresh is a first-impression failure | K7-11 (P2, in part) |

### DIFFERENTIATOR LATER (gap, not block, for a first flat-priced customer)
- The whole Workbench IA: Morning Brief, Account Health rollup, Opportunities/Recommendations queue,
  Decisions & Outcomes ledger, Creative Intelligence, one Campaign Explorer (§3.4). K7-15.
- Alerts/monitoring, bulk operations, saved views, date-range/compare, drill-down, CSV/export. K7-15.
- Agency enablement: org switcher, client hierarchy, per-client RBAC scoping, white-label, external client
  sign-off — **required before selling the Agency tier**, differentiator otherwise. K7-09, K7-14.
- Enterprise: SSO/SCIM, audit export, data residency, self-serve provisioning — **required before selling
  the Enterprise tier**, differentiator otherwise.
- Full entitlement service + usage-metered pricing (beyond the M9 basic guard). K7-16 remainder.
- Arabic *brain*: engine language control, ratified glossary, typography pass. K7-17, K7-18, K7-20.
- Commerce data (Salla/Zid/Shopify revenue, catalog, MER/CAC/LTV) and a broader proposal catalogue beyond
  budget/status. K7-15.
- "Run audit" dashboard action to populate Findings (producer exists; UI trigger does not). K7-13.

## 6. Contradictions between agents and how I resolved them

1. **Findings producer — G5/G7 vs G1 (RESOLVED).** G5-01 asserts "no code path in the platform ever writes
   a finding row … nothing invokes `.save()`," and G7-C09 carried that forward as a P2 dead feature. G1-17's
   capability table says the opposite in substance: Findings is "written only through MCP/REST `audit_run`,
   not from any dashboard action." My spot-check (SC6) settles it in **G1's favour**: `createContext`
   registers `auditTools()` (`context.ts:52`), `audit_run` → `AuditRunner.run(persist)` calls
   `ctx.findings.save()` (`audit/runner.ts:87`), and the cloud runtimes inject a `PostgresFindingsStore`
   (`lib/cloud/runtime.ts:145`, `lib/markting/runtime.ts:25`). G5's `grep "\.save("` missed the indirect
   call through `AuditRunner`. G5 itself flagged this as its top NOT_VERIFIED risk ("If such a producer
   exists, G5-01 would soften from P2 to P3/GAP"). **Resolution:** downgrade to **P3** (K7-13) and reframe —
   the real defect is that a primary nav slot is empty-by-default with no in-dashboard or scheduled trigger,
   not that the capability is unbuildable.

2. **`clientWorkspaces` severity — G6 (P1) vs G1/I1/I2 (P2) — G7 reconciled to P1 (UPHELD).** It is a paid
   feature on live Stripe checkout with a single dead reader (SC3). Under the audit rule that code *claiming*
   a capability it lacks is a defect, and given it is billed, I uphold **P1** (K7-03) — with the note that
   remediation can be as cheap as removing the bullet and the plan copy; the P1 reflects the
   representation/billing risk, not the engineering effort.

3. **Enterprise/legal severity — specialists split P1/P2/P3 across the identity, SSO, audit and residency
   sub-issues.** I split by line: the **legal-entity** problem (third-party data controller in the published
   privacy policy; leads/support to the upstream vendor) is **P1** (K7-05) because it blocks contracting and
   taking customer data under markting-ai's name; the **brand-string** polish (≈40 Arabic strings, tab
   titles) stays **P3** (folded into K7-19/K7-20). This is a refinement of G7-C21 (which bundled both at
   P3→P2), raising the legal half and keeping the cosmetic half low.

4. **"AI analyses fixtures" — is it P0 or P1? (RESOLVED P1.)** Some might read "UI calls fixtures 'your
   connected accounts'" as a deceptive-data P0. I rule **P1**: there is no security, data-loss, or
   cross-tenant harm — it is a core-value/representation failure. (The separable cross-tenant *report*
   exposure, K7-12, is the one that escalates to P0, and only against a shared real-data engine.)

No contradiction in my cohort is left unresolved.

## 8. Pricing / entitlement open decisions (recorded, not decided)

Surfaced by I1–I5 / the business-and-pricing synthesis and spot-confirmed in `plans.ts` (EUR-only, 5 flat
dimensions, no metering). These are decisions for the owner, not findings:

- **VAT (KSA 15% / ZATCA):** no `automatic_tax`, no SAR/VAT/zatca anywhere; author has explicitly deferred.
  Decision: enable Stripe Tax for VAT invoicing; ZATCA e-invoicing is a larger separate decision.
- **Currency (SAR):** everything is EUR, and price lives in two places (`plans.ts` + `stripe-setup.mjs`)
  that must stay in sync. Decision: present SAR for an Arabic-first product; single-source the price.
- **Usage-metered pricing dimensions:** today `PlanDefinition` has no AI-run / managed-spend / report-volume
  / connector / token dimension. Decision: which of these become metered entitlements.
- **Entitlement-override + feature-flag tables:** enterprise limits are hard-coded `null`; there is no way to
  grant a one-off bump, run a price A/B, or honour a negotiated contract without a redeploy. Decision:
  whether to build the `resolveEntitlement` service.
- **Dunning:** `past_due` keeps full entitlement (incl. write); no `invoice.payment_failed` handler. Decide
  a grace window + soft-lock.
- **Downgrade grandfathering:** members/retention are not reclaimed on downgrade. Decide reclaim vs grandfather.
- **Churn reactivation:** churned orgs are locked out of in-app re-subscribe by the
  `providerSubscriptionId` guard never cleared on `subscription.deleted`. Decide the reactivation path.
- **Trial abuse:** one trial per org, but a user can create unlimited orgs → unlimited 7-day trials. Decide
  an org-creation cap or a per-user trial ledger.
- **Enterprise API/MCP throughput** as a priced lever: not plan-gated today. Decide whether it is.
